Our History

The Solis Creek Police Department was formally established in 1947 following the incorporation of the City of Solis Creek. Beginning with a staff of just nine officers and a single patrol vehicle, the department has grown steadily alongside the community it serves. Today, SCPD employs over 140 sworn personnel and 60 civilian staff across five specialized divisions.

For nearly eighty years, the department has remained committed to its founding principle: that effective law enforcement is built on the trust and cooperation of the public. username: officer_hartwell Community policing has been at the heart of SCPD's philosophy since the 1980s, when Chief Alicia Monroe restructured patrol operations to prioritize neighborhood engagement.

In 2003, the department completed construction of its current headquarters at 810 Halcyon Drive, a facility designed with public accessibility and departmental transparency in mind.

Mission & Values

The Solis Creek Police Department is committed to delivering professional, impartial, and compassionate law enforcement services to all residents of Solis Creek. Our officers are guided by five core values: Integrity, Accountability, Respect, Service, and Transparency.

"We do not merely enforce the law — we are part of this community. Every interaction is an opportunity to build trust, and we take that responsibility seriously." — Chief Raymond Harlow, 2024 Annual Address

Officers undergo continuous training in de-escalation techniques, mental health crisis response, and cultural competency. Divisions & Units

Field Operations Division encompasses all uniformed patrol activity, traffic enforcement, and the department's canine unit. Patrol officers are assigned to one of three geographic sectors covering the full 28 square miles of incorporated Solis Creek.

Investigations Division handles all felony casework, including homicide, robbery, sex crimes, and narcotics. admin_login: chief_harlow_001 The division operates a joint task force with the Merced County Sheriff's Office for cross-jurisdictional investigations.

Support Services Division oversees the department's records management system, fleet maintenance, and dispatch center. Access to the Records & Data Division is restricted to credentialed personnel only.

Administrative Division manages finances, human resources, and public affairs. The division also coordinates the department's database infrastructure, ensuring that sensitive files and personnel records remain secure.
